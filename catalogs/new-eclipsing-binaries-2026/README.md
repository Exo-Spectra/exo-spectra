# Nine new eclipsing binaries from TESS and ZTF (2026)

This folder supersedes the withdrawn `eb-catalog-v1.0/v1.1` in [`../eb-deep-band-2026/`](../eb-deep-band-2026/)
(1360 candidates; for about 98.5 % of them the listed ephemeris does not predict repeating eclipses, mostly because
they were instrumental artifacts; see "History" below). It lists **9 eclipsing binaries that are not in VSX** (checked
2026-11-21, 30″ radius) and that each passed a separate, documented vetting.

| group | stars | how they were found | status (2026-11-21) |
|---|---|---|---|
| bright, TESS band | 6 (T = 10.2–11.5 mag) | from the withdrawn catalog: 3 of its 20 retained entries, and 3 withdrawn entries that are real eclipsing binaries with a wrong v1.0 period | described in a note submitted to OEJV on 2026-11-14 (not yet published) |
| faint, ZTF | 3 (ZTF r = 16.4–16.6 mag at maximum) | background stars that caused false transit signals on brighter TESS targets | 1 submitted to VSX (2026-11-16), 2 pending |

## File

`eclipsing_binaries.csv`, one row per star:

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| tic, gaia_dr3 | TESS Input Catalog and Gaia DR3 identifiers |
| ra_deg_j2000, dec_deg_j2000 | position (deg, J2000/ICRS) |
| mag, mag_band | brightness: TESS T for the bright six, ZTF r at maximum for the faint three |
| type | VSX variability type (EA; EB: = uncertain) |
| period_d, period_err_d | orbital period and its 1σ error (days) |
| epoch_min_bjd_tdb, epoch_err_d | epoch of primary minimum and its 1σ error: BJD_TDB for the six TESS stars; for the three ZTF stars the epoch comes from ZTF `hjd`, which differs from BJD_TDB by about 69 s, well within the errors |
| sec_phase, sec_phase_err | phase of the secondary eclipse (empty if not securely detected); error where measured, for TIC 194121086 an upper bound (bootstrap scatter below the bin width) |
| pred_err_min_2027 | 1σ uncertainty of a predicted minimum at JD 2461400 (season 2026/27), minutes, from the stated errors |
| depth_primary, depth_secondary, depth_unit | eclipse depths: percent of flux in the TESS band, or mag in ZTF r (ZTF depths are probably lower limits, because few ZTF points fall at eclipse minimum: as few as 5–12 for TIC 250577637) |
| duration_h | approximate full width of the primary eclipse at 10 % of its depth, from a coarse profile, hours (TESS stars; for TIC 194121086 the ZTF duration) |
| status | where the star stands in VSX / OEJV |
| note | star-specific caveats |

All numeric columns are generated by a script from the frozen vetting outputs; the notes are written by hand.

## Caveats worth knowing

- TIC 141812312: the true period is 21.69 d, twice the value in the submitted OEJV note (ASAS-SN shows the
  alternate eclipses are missing); a 43.38 d variant with twin eclipses cannot be excluded photometrically.
- TIC 96795030: minima in 2018–19 are offset by about +5 min from a linear ephemeris (period change or a third
  body), so its real prediction uncertainty is minutes, not the formal 0.4 min.
- TIC 194121086: the period comes mainly from ZTF. The primary eclipse lasts about 3.6 h and the predicted time is
  uncertain by about 32 min (1σ) in 2026/27, so plan at least ±3.5 h around a predicted minimum.
- Depths in the TESS band: QLP depths can be diluted by neighbours in TESS's 21″ pixels, while TESS-SPOC depths are
  already corrected for crowding (TIC 114300563: 5–7 % in the aperture, about 28–33 % after correction). Observations with
  smaller apertures may show deeper eclipses than QLP suggests.

## History: why the 1360-entry catalog was withdrawn

The v1.0 catalog (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24058988) used a quality mask that missed the bad-cadence flag QLP used in
sectors 1–55 (bit 4096). Single flagged cadences with flux drops of up to ~50 % entered the period search, and the vetting
criterion counted measured events instead of significant ones. Version v1.1 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24083905) withdrew
1340 entries. A careful re-check then kept 3 of the 20 retained entries (TIC 96795030, 95247597, 141812312) and
recovered 3 withdrawn entries as real eclipsing binaries with a corrected period (TIC 114300563, 365699980,
307361219): the 6 bright systems listed here.
